Important defects found and fixed:
A. Adaptive Edge queried ORDER ASC LIMIT N, so a long history could exclude the newest trades. Fixed to DESC LIMIT N then chronological reversal.
B. Adaptive Edge synchronous SQLite had timeout=10 on event-loop paths. Bounded to 250ms and cached 30s.
C. Snapshot coherence used ANY observable timeframe. Changed to ALL required frames observable for production final validation.
D. Frame coherence did not explicitly reject duplicate/non-monotonic open_time. Added.
E. Derivative timing could accept zero duration as valid metadata. Positive duration is now mandatory; optional endpoints are checked for consistency.
F. Execution depth tolerated up to 2% unfilled requested notional. Tightened to 0.5%.
G. Invalid side could fall into SHORT-side book logic. Explicit LONG/SHORT validation added.
H. Several synchronous DB report callbacks still occupied PTB's sequential update handler even when work was in to_thread. They now ACK and detach immediately.
I. Detached UI tasks are cancelled/awaited on shutdown to avoid resource leakage.
