2. DECISION MARGIN
- Measures how far PRIME is separated from the next-best already-qualified Production candidate.

- Margin is attached to the signal feature snapshot for later audit/replay.

3. EARLY WATCH
- Dedicated Telegram panel showing Fast Radar focus, recent checks, ARMED candidates and breaking-news focus.
- EARLY WATCH is explicitly not an entry instruction.
- Existing 60-second Fast Radar and 30-second ENTRY NOW confirmation remain unchanged.

4. FUTURES L2
