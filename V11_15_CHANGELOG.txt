V11.15.0 · STRONG SIGNALS

Purpose
- Increase selectivity of real AUTO / ENTRY NOW signals without inflating scores.
- Distinguish a merely qualified candidate from broad independent consensus.

Strong Consensus Gate
- Negative-only overlay; never increases professional_rank and never rescues rejected setups.
- AUTO / ENTRY NOW requires: PRO >=84, >=5 independent evidence families, <=1 soft conflict,
  clean enough fresh execution, no hard conflict, no mature negative forward-edge blocker.
- PRIME STRONG requires: PRO >=88, >=6 independent families, zero conflicts, stable/non-fragile
  selection, and no NEAR TIE label.
- Meta OOS participates only after Meta has independently reached READY.
- Robust positive history can strengthen the confidence label but never the trading rank.

Delivery behaviour
- Merely QUALIFIED_ONLY candidates are not armed for production ENTRY NOW.
- STRONG candidates may enter the normal 30-second ENTRY NOW persistence machine.
- Only PRIME_STRONG may receive the asynchronous №1 PRIME label.
- Every final trigger is rechecked through Strong Consensus after the full fresh Production rebuild.

UI
- Signal cards expose Strong Consensus label and score.
- PRIME branding is reserved for PRIME_STRONG; a near-tie cannot masquerade as a clear №1.

No profitability guarantee. This release intentionally trades frequency for stronger evidence quality.
