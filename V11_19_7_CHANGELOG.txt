KORKOVTS SIGNAL AI V11.19.7 · FULL-UNIVERSE SIGNAL ENGINE

Why this release exists
The previous production funnel could reduce 145-170 liquid Futures symbols to 1-2
before derivatives analysis, and Spot could blanket-veto BUYs because slow 1D regime,
auxiliary news/crowding degradation or a second-stage 4H gate.

FUTURES
- Every liquid Binance USDT-M perpetual receives 15m/1H/4H discovery and independent LONG/SHORT rank.
- Heavy derivatives analysis uses a balanced 36-symbol shortlist (top LONG + top SHORT), not only technical survivors.
- V11.18 Strong/Indicator/Adaptive/Portfolio/Final-Risk/ENTRY-NOW remains authoritative.
- Real bad taker/order-flow, execution, extreme crowding, negative events and invalid geometry still block.
- Binance clock guard now uses 3 samples + lowest RTT and recent-good fallback for transient /time failures.

SPOT
- Every liquid Binance Spot/USDT symbol receives daily/cross-sectional ranking.
- Deep pool widened to 36 symbols.
- Removed the pre-L2 second-stage 4H kill switch.
- Slow BEAR regime can no longer blanket-block an independently strong recovery leader.
- Missing auxiliary Futures crowding/news telemetry is uncertainty/penalty, not directional proof.
- Actual negative news, extreme crowding, poor execution, weak flow, bad price location and evidence conflict remain vetoes.
- Existing Spot watchtower/delivery/risk logic remains in the V11.18 runtime.

IMPORTANT
This release is designed to stop false 'no signal' caused by architecture.
It does NOT guarantee profitable trades and does not fabricate an entry just to satisfy a quota.

CODE QUALITY AUDIT
- Removed stale direct production-function imports.
- Adaptive deep shortlist avoids artificial 50/50 LONG/SHORT allocation.
- Final Spot delivery now matches discovery policy for telemetry-only degradation.
- Added behavioral shortlist and delivery-parity regression contracts.

SCAN-LOCK FIX
- The 175s scan target is now an actual deadline.
- Frame batches are individually bounded and pending frame tasks are cancelled.
- Deep derivatives tasks are cancelled at the overall deadline.
- AUTO lock wait is aligned to the bounded full-scan budget instead of 90 seconds.
- Running scans publish live progress; low coverage is reported as incomplete, not "0 signals".

SIGNAL RECOVERY
- Strong Futures geometry-only rejections become controlled pullback candidates.
- Existing ENTRY NOW remains authoritative before delivery.
- Spot news outage is degraded, not a whole-scan abort.
- Real strategy audit is retained in diagnostics.

API RESILIENCE
- Full-universe research traffic is capped below critical health/ENTRY capacity.
- Research waits proactively near Binance request-weight ceiling instead of driving into 429.
- Health reports real rate-limit cooldown rather than fake 9999ms/+1000s sentinel metrics.
- A short transient health-probe failure may reuse a very recent verified state as DEGRADED, never as fresh market evidence.
- Futures frame/deep concurrency reduced slightly while preserving all-symbol ranking and the 36-name deep shortlist.

SOURCE-STAGE RESILIENCE
- exchangeInfo and ticker/24hr are no longer hidden inside one generic 30s gather.
- Every mandatory source has its own timeout and exact Telegram diagnostic.
- exchangeInfo may reuse a recently verified 6h cache; ticker/24hr may reuse only a
  short 75s verified cache because it drives liquidity/ranking.
- exchangeInfo and ticker/24hr are critical-priority governor requests.
- Heavy analysis concurrency defaults to 6, leaving production headroom.
- A real Binance cooldown is reported explicitly instead of generic TimeoutError.
- A source-stage failure cannot masquerade as "0 liquid markets / no signals".
