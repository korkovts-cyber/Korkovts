KORKOVTS SIGNAL AI V11.21.6 · FULL-UNIVERSE SIGNAL ENGINE

Why this release exists
The previous production funnel could reduce 145-170 liquid Futures symbols to 1-2
before derivatives analysis, and Spot could blanket-veto BUYs because slow 1D regime,
auxiliary news/crowding degradation or a second-stage 4H gate.

FUTURES
- Every liquid Binance USDT-M perpetual receives 15m/1H/4H discovery and independent LONG/SHORT rank.
- Heavy derivatives analysis uses a balanced 36-symbol shortlist (top LONG + top SHORT), not only technical survivors.
- V11.18 Strong/Indicator/Adaptive/Portfolio/Final-Risk/ENTRY-NOW remains authoritative.
- Real bad taker/order-flow, execution, extreme crowding, negative events and invalid geometry still block.
- Binance clock guard now uses 3 samples + lowest RTT and recent-good fallback for transient /time failures.

SPOT
- Every liquid Binance Spot/USDT symbol receives daily/cross-sectional ranking.
- Deep pool widened to 36 symbols.
- Removed the pre-L2 second-stage 4H kill switch.
- Slow BEAR regime can no longer blanket-block an independently strong recovery leader.
- Missing auxiliary Futures crowding/news telemetry is uncertainty/penalty, not directional proof.
- Actual negative news, extreme crowding, poor execution, weak flow, bad price location and evidence conflict remain vetoes.
- Existing Spot watchtower/delivery/risk logic remains in the V11.18 runtime.

IMPORTANT
This release is designed to stop false 'no signal' caused by architecture.
It does NOT guarantee profitable trades and does not fabricate an entry just to satisfy a quota.

CODE QUALITY AUDIT
- Removed stale direct production-function imports.
- Adaptive deep shortlist avoids artificial 50/50 LONG/SHORT allocation.
- Final Spot delivery now matches discovery policy for telemetry-only degradation.
- Added behavioral shortlist and delivery-parity regression contracts.

SCAN-LOCK FIX
- The 175s scan target is now an actual deadline.
- Frame batches are individually bounded and pending frame tasks are cancelled.
- Deep derivatives tasks are cancelled at the overall deadline.
- AUTO lock wait is aligned to the bounded full-scan budget instead of 90 seconds.
- Running scans publish live progress; low coverage is reported as incomplete, not "0 signals".

SIGNAL RECOVERY
- Strong Futures geometry-only rejections become controlled pullback candidates.
- Existing ENTRY NOW remains authoritative before delivery.
- Spot news outage is degraded, not a whole-scan abort.
- Real strategy audit is retained in diagnostics.

API RESILIENCE
- Full-universe research traffic is capped below critical health/ENTRY capacity.
- Research waits proactively near Binance request-weight ceiling instead of driving into 429.
- Health reports real rate-limit cooldown rather than fake 9999ms/+1000s sentinel metrics.
- A short transient health-probe failure may reuse a very recent verified state as DEGRADED, never as fresh market evidence.
- Futures frame/deep concurrency reduced slightly while preserving all-symbol ranking and the 36-name deep shortlist.

SOURCE-STAGE RESILIENCE
- exchangeInfo and ticker/24hr are no longer hidden inside one generic 30s gather.
- Every mandatory source has its own timeout and exact Telegram diagnostic.
- exchangeInfo may reuse a recently verified 6h cache; ticker/24hr may reuse only a
  short 75s verified cache because it drives liquidity/ranking.
- exchangeInfo and ticker/24hr are critical-priority governor requests.
- Heavy analysis concurrency defaults to 6, leaving production headroom.
- A real Binance cooldown is reported explicitly instead of generic TimeoutError.
- A source-stage failure cannot masquerade as "0 liquid markets / no signals".

TWO-STAGE DEEP ENGINE
- All liquid USDT-M perpetuals still receive the full multi-timeframe market ranking.
- The wide 36-name shortlist now receives a cheap derivatives screen using one bulk
  premiumIndex request plus one OI request per symbol.
- Only the best 14 screened candidates receive the expensive full 9-component
  derivatives snapshot.
- Full-deep direction allocation is adaptive with opposite-side reserve, not 50/50.
- The fast screen cannot generate or deliver a signal; all final candidates still
  pass the existing production strategy, Strong/Indicator/Adaptive/Final Risk and ENTRY NOW.
- This removes the impossible 36 x full-snapshot workload from the ~3 minute scan budget.

TRUTHFUL TELEGRAM DIAGNOSTICS
- Manual PRIME FUTURES no longer maps every exception to "mandatory source unavailable".
- Source timeout, rate-limit cooldown, fast-screen coverage and full-deep deadline failures
  are reported with their real stage/reason.
- FAST Futures receives the same truthful diagnostics.
- Safety behavior remains fail-closed: diagnostics improve, trading gates do not weaken.

V11.21.6 DATA ARCHITECTURE
- Shared REST pacing: default 7 research requests/sec.
- Single-flight deduplicates identical concurrent Binance calls.
- Short TTL reuse is limited to derivatives telemetry; depth/candles/time are not cached here.
- Proactive request-weight ceiling lowered from 1900 to 1500; analysis concurrency lowered to 5.
- Spot Watchtower reuses a full Futures crowding snapshot for 150 seconds between adjacent checks.
- Real 429/418 cooldown still produces PRODUCTION HEALTH PAUSE.
- Trading thresholds and live BUY/ENTRY gates are unchanged.

STABILITY AUDIT
- Fixed cancellation safety in REST single-flight: a scan deadline can no longer
  leave followers waiting on an unresolved Future.

  consumer cannot cancel data needed by other tasks.
- Live /depth, /aggTrades and /bookTicker are realtime-priority and no longer wait
  behind the bulk research pacing queue.
- Realtime priority still passes through the underlying Binance governor,
  global concurrency limit and real 429/418 cooldown.
- Spot WATCH persistence remains SQLite-backed on DATABASE_PATH; restart/deploy
  does not erase an active watchlist when /data is persistent.

FINAL STABILITY AUDIT
- AUTO performs Production Health preflight before a full 180-symbol Futures scan.
  Hard PAUSE/cooldown skips the research cycle instead of feeding the cooldown loop.

- Manual SPOT · SWING reports the actual scanner reason instead of a generic source-outage message.

V11.21.6 FRAME ARCHITECTURE FIX
- Root cause confirmed from production: 491 observed symbols x 3 kline timeframes = 1473 frame requests were being attempted before ranking.
- At the safe 4.5 research req/s this workload needs >5 minutes, while the old frame stage allowed only 90 seconds; production observed 134/491 (27%), exactly matching the throughput ceiling.
- Stage 1A now analyzes one primary candle timeframe for every ACTIONABLE/liquid symbol (~202), while all observed symbols remain represented by exchange/ticker liquidity telemetry.
- Stage 1B sends only a balanced top 72 LONG/SHORT opportunities to the other two timeframes.
- Typical frame request count falls from ~1473 to ~346 before derivatives analysis.
- Primary liquid coverage must be >=90%; multiframe shortlist coverage must be >=85%.
- Then 72 multi-TF -> 36 fast derivatives -> 14 full derivatives -> Production -> ARMED/ENTRY.
- No live safety gate, Spot BUY gate or ENTRY NOW confirmation was removed.
