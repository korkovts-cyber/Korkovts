V11.22.0 DEEP AUDIT

Additional confirmed state-machine bugs fixed:

1) BUY READY remained partly self-cancelling in NEUTRAL:
   the lane that created BUY accepts score>=78 / RS>=70 after its own hard
   safety checks, but V11.21.9 still used 80/75 downstream. V11.22.0 aligns
   downstream to 78/70 only for a candidate already marked BUY by that lane.
   Independent-evidence penalties and all hard vetoes still apply.

2) A 15-minute broad Spot WATCH refresh could erase a local-L2 READY 1/2:
   broad discovery calls upsert(WATCH), whose legacy SQL resets confirm_streak.
   V11.22.0 preserves exactly the existing 1/2 only for materially unchanged
   geometry. A fresh 1-minute watchtower revalidation returning WATCH still
   calls reset_spot_ready and immediately clears the proof.

No forced trades. No synthetic 2/2. Futures/API fixes from V11.21.7,
Spot geometry persistence from V11.21.8, and READY/L2 priority from V11.21.9
remain active.
